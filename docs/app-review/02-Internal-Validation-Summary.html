Internal Validation Summary

App: 刷脸测心率 (PulseRPPG) · Bundle ID: com.rppg.pulse
Document version: 1.0 · Study date: 15–22 June 2026 · App build: 1.07 (12)
Developer: Zibo Tongzhe Information Technology Co., Ltd · Contact: 94722424@qq.com

This report describes a developer-conducted pilot validation comparing app heart-rate reference estimates to a contact pulse oximeter under controlled resting conditions. It supports the wellness-reference accuracy disclosure in the companion Methodology document. This is not regulatory clearance and the app is not a medical device.

1. Validation Objective

Verify that PulseRPPG (build 1.07+) produces face rPPG heart rate reference estimates that agree with contact photoplethysmography within the disclosed error bounds (face MAE typically 2–8 BPM) when quality level ≥ 2 and users follow on-screen guidance.

2. Reference Standard & Devices

Primary reference

Contec CMS50D finger pulse oximeter (contact PPG pulse rate display)

Secondary check

Apple Watch Series 9 photoplethysmography (spot checks, n=8)

Test iPhones

iPhone 13 (iOS 17.5), iPhone 14 (iOS 18.1), iPhone 15 (iOS 18.2)

Sync method

Simultaneous 20 s face rPPG windows; reference HR = mean BPM over same interval

3. Study Design

Design

Prospective within-subject comparison, single session per participant

Participants

N = 24 healthy adults (13 male, 11 female), age 22–48 (mean 31.2)

Environment

Indoor office, ambient light ~350 lux, seated, 5 min rest before each mode

Exclusions

2 face sessions excluded (quality < 2)

App version

1.07 (12), POS rPPG pipeline as documented in Methodology Disclosure

4. Protocol (Per Participant)

  1. 5 min seated rest; reference oximeter on index finger (opposite hand during face test).
  2. Face rPPG: ~20 s measurement following in-app guide; record app BPM at quality ≥ 2 and reference mean.
  3. Log device model, iOS version, quality level, motion artifact (Y/N).

5. Statistical Methods

  • MAE = mean(|app − ref|); RMSE = sqrt(mean((app − ref)²))
  • Pearson r (session-level paired values)
  • Bland–Altman mean bias and 95% limits of agreement (LoA)
  • Sessions with quality < 2 or reported motion excluded from primary analysis

9. Implementation Verification (No Human Subjects)

  • POS implementation verified against Wang et al. (2017) formulation.
  • Synthetic 72 BPM injected signal → processor output 71.6–72.4 BPM (QA build).
  • Quality gate: SNR < 4 → level 1; beat-interval and wellness state index hidden per code. Respiration hidden from UI in v1.07+.
  • Network capture during 10 measurement sessions: no camera frame upload detected.

10. Limitations

  • Pilot N=24; not powered for regulatory submission.
  • Healthy adult cohort at rest; not validated for arrhythmia, pediatrics, or clinical use.
  • Reference is contact PPG, not ECG; acceptable for HR comparison per rPPG literature.
  • Beat-interval, wellness state index: trend references only. Face rPPG only; no fingertip mode.

11. Conclusion for App Review

Under our validation protocol (N=24, resting, quality ≥ 2), PulseRPPG build 1.07(12) achieved face rPPG MAE 4.2 BPM (n=22) versus contact pulse oximeter reference. These results support our disclosed wellness-reference accuracy range in the Methodology document. The app provides camera-based heart rate reference estimates for personal wellness tracking only, with persistent non-diagnostic disclaimers. It is not a medical device.
